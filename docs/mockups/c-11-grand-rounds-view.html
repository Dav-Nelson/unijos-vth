Discussion point

Microsporum canis is the most common cause of dermatophytosis in dogs in Nigeria. Wood's lamp examination is a quick screen but has limited sensitivity (~50% of Microsporum canis strains fluoresce). DTM culture remains the gold standard. What alternative antifungal would you use if the owner cannot afford Itraconazole?
